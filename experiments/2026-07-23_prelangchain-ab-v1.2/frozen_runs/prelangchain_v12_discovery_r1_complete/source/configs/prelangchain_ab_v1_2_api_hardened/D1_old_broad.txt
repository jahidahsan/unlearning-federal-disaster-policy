BROAD HISTORICAL OPERATIONALIZATION

Organizational unlearning is evidenced when a government organization reconsiders, discards, realigns, or merges established knowledge, assumptions, routines, roles, policies, capabilities, resources, or organizational arrangements after experience or failure.

Qualifying modes include epistemic reconsidering, normative discarding, technical realignment, and integrative merging. A passage may be positive even when it describes strategic integration, revised coordination, changed roles, technical adaptation, or additive institutional change rather than literal removal. Diagnosis alone is insufficient, but explicit words such as abandon or replace are not required.
